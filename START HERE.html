Open it. Set it up once. Fish.

1 · Open the fishing app

Double-click Start Fishing.cmd in this folder. On the first launch it installs any missing dependencies. The app opens in Normal fishing, with your original settings tabs.

2 · Guided setup, once

Open WoW at your fishing spot. Enable Auto Loot in WoW and put your Fishing ability on a visible action bar.

Click Guided setup. Switch to WoW and cast during the ten-second countdown.

A frozen picture opens. There is no time limit:

  1. Draw a box around the water where your bobber lands. Click Next.
  2. Draw a tight box around your bobber. Click Next.
  3. Click your Fishing ability icon on the picture, confirm Auto Loot, and click Save setup.

The app saves the area, bobber picture and casting location together. No coordinates, HWND, profiles or model settings are needed. Cancel keeps your previous settings.

3 · Start fishing

Check Enable live game controls when you want the app to actually fish. Click Start fishing.

Leave the checkbox off for a dry-run with no game input. F9 pauses/resumes and Esc stops fishing. Let the game remain in front while it runs.

Next time, just open Start Fishing and start. Redo guided setup if the game window, camera, action bars or UI scale changes.

Normal fishing contains the original timing, safety, bait, session, image and location settings. Separate top-level tabs provide Visual targeting and Neural training. What each variable does and how to train your neural detector. Start Targeting.cmd remains the developer preview launcher.
